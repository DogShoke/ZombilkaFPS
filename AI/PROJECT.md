# Zombilka FPS

New first-person zombie shooter. Current milestone is a single-player combat sandbox focused on movement, pistol feel, and zombies. Progression, inventory, monetization, and menus are out of scope.

Rojo syncs `src/shared` to `ReplicatedStorage.Shared`, `src/client` to `StarterPlayerScripts.Client`, and `src/server` to `ServerScriptService.Server`.
